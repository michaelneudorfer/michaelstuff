Luna's Sunbeam Kingdom

Luna was a small grey cat who ruled a kingdom that moved across the living room floor every day.

In the morning, her kingdom sat by the front window. By noon, it had drifted to the rug. In the late afternoon, it climbed onto the sofa, and Luna followed it loyally, one patch of warm light at a time.

One cloudy day, the kingdom vanished entirely. Luna searched under the table, behind the curtains, and even inside a shoebox. It was nowhere.

Then her human lit the fireplace. Luna blinked, stretched, and settled before the flames. A new kingdom, she decided, warmer than the old one. Queens adapt.
